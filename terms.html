The app

Raise helps you raise and keep a sourdough starter. It offers guidance, not guarantees: starters vary with flour, water and room, and the forecast is an estimate from what you log.

Food safety

You are responsible for what you eat. If a starter shows fuzzy mould or pink or orange streaks, throw it away and start again, whatever the app says.
