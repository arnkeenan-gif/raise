What it does

One minute a day.
A loaf on day ten.

A starter is flour, water and patience. Raise takes care of the patience part.

Feed once a day. The rabbit keeps count.

Tap today when you've fed it. The ring counts down to the next feed, the week fills in, and your streak grows.

  • Reminders at the time you choose
  • Counter or fridge, one feed a day or a week
  • Streak savers for the day you forget

Snap the jar. It fills in the rest.

Take a photo and the rabbit reads the rise, the bubbles, the surface and how lively it looks, straight into your day. The smell is the only thing left to you.

  • Rise, bubbles and activity read from a photo
  • A day-by-day photo diary
  • Ask anything about your starter, any time

A forecast for the day it's ready.

Flour, room warmth and your feeds together tell Raise when your starter will double reliably. The health score explains itself.

  • Health score with the reasons behind it
  • Ready-to-bake day, updated as you log
  • Rise, warmth, feeds and smell over two weeks

Dress the rabbit. Change the view.

Hats, glasses, aprons and fur colours, and nine places to stand, from a wheat field to the mill at night. The home-screen widgets follow.

  • Nine backgrounds, eight fur colours
  • Hats, glasses and outfits that fit every expression
  • Frosted-glass widgets with the live countdown

How it works

Ten days, start to bake.

Raise walks you through it the first time and stays out of the way after that.

Meet your rabbit

Name it, pick how it talks to you, and answer a few questions so the plan fits how you bake.

Mix on day one

Flour, water, a clean jar. Tell Raise which flour and how warm the spot is; it works out the rest.

Feed and snap

A minute a day. Tap the feed and take a photo; the rabbit fills in what it sees.

Bake on day ten

When it doubles within a few hours of a feed, it's ready. Log the loaf and keep going.
