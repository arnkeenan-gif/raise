Privacy Notice

Last updated 18 September 2026.

What Raise keeps on your phone

Your rabbit's name, your onboarding answers, your feed log, the readings you enter, your photos and your settings are stored on your device. Raise has no account and no server of its own, and does not send this data anywhere on its own.

What leaves your phone

If you use "Ask Rocky", the question you type, the photo you attach and a short summary of your starter (name, day, recent readings) are sent to the AI service that answers, and are used only to produce that answer. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

Purchases

Raise Plus is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; Raise only learns whether a subscription is active.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone and never pass through a server.

Analytics and tracking

Raise contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Deleting your data

Settings → Log out and clear this phone removes everything Raise stored. Deleting the app does the same.
